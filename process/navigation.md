Check A of T5c observed it once more in the host, without a hint: half applied, the plan was judged, the goal was not reviewed (node 7 skipped, the next objective proposed straight after the result). Three observations in a row; the gap is stable. Since 2026-10-13 that cause is under test: Nexus 1.6.0 delivers the algorithm's text and the saved position at every prompt.

- Whether the agent, after a negative result, evaluates its objective against the Project Goal and determines the next step on its own (check A of T5c, reworded 2026-10-11: **partial**, the next step yes and grounded, the evaluation against the Project Goal no), and whether it prevents a departure from the goal without the operator's prompt (check B of T5c: **observed once, positive, 2026-10-10**; whether it holds in ordinary sessions without a test remains to be seen).

- Where the counter rests between tasks (5.2 after each 5.4, as the host's agent chose, or a direct continuation of the approved plan); observation only, the algorithm is not changed on one case.
- Whether the host's T1 closes on the cold reading of a second person within a useful time: **answered 2026-10-11, it does not close that way**; the reading was negative and the criterion itself was judged unfit by the operator.
- Whether a hook change in Nexus needs one live-check round per lifecycle event it touches (T8 needed three where one was planned).
- Whether steering as agent behaviour keeps holding without a gate, or rots into ritual.
- Whether a net of this shape stays one screen and stays current once the project has more than one line of work.
- Whether the cycle applies to projects with a known path; only the research case has been tried.
